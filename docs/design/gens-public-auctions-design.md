# GENS — System Design: Public Auctions (§6.58, new)
*Polish and expansion pass. The real, distinct commercial cousin of Public Contracts & Competitive Bidding — that document built locatio-conductio publica, the state letting its own business out to bid; this document builds the auctio, the ordinary real Roman institution for competitively selling goods, property, and people at public sale, run by a hired praeco rather than a Censor. This pass adds real multi-lot Auction Days with a genuine sequential momentum effect, absentee bidding through an agent for a household that can't attend in person, and — the honest, dishonest half of any real auction house — two real, symmetrical cheating options: the Ring (buyers secretly colluding to suppress the price) and Puffing (a seller's own shill bid inflating it), both with genuine Discovery risk and consequence. A real post-Reserve negotiation option and the Praeco's own real commission round out the picture.*

## 1. Scope & Role — The Real Auctio, Distinct from a State Contract

Public Contracts & Competitive Bidding built the real institution behind Rome letting its own business out to the highest-standing bidder, run by a Censor. This document builds its real, distinct commercial cousin: the ordinary auctio, run by a hired praeco (auctioneer) rather than a state magistrate. The two institutions share the same underlying logic — a competitive bid, evaluated on more than price alone, resolved to a winner — and this document deliberately reuses that document's own bid-evaluation formula rather than inventing a second one.

---

## 2. The Auction's Anatomy

Every Auction is built from four real components: the **Lot**, the **Reserve Price**, the **Bidders**, and the **Praeco**.

## 3. Auction Triggers — Where a Lot Actually Comes From

Five real, distinct sources: **Slave Market Auction Day**, **Sub Hasta Sale**, **Bonorum Venditio**, **Estate Liquidation**, and **A Unique Lot Coming to Market** — see §7–9 for the last three's own real depth.

---

## 4. Bidding — Reusing the Public Contracts Shape

No new formula: this document reuses Public Contracts & Competitive Bidding's own three-input bid evaluation directly — Price, Reliability, and Influence — resolving to a single winner exactly the way that document's own bidding section already specifies.

---

## 5. Multi-Lot Auction Days — A Real Sequential Momentum

New this pass, and a genuine, real texture worth naming: an Auction Day is rarely one single Lot — a Bonorum Venditio in particular typically liquidates an entire estate's own property, goods, and enslaved staff across many successive Lots in one real event. This document adds a real, honest sequential effect: a household that visibly wins an early Lot at a high price reads to everyone still present as flush and eager, and can find later Lots in the same Auction Day drawing sharper, more aggressive competing bids as a direct result — a real, human social dynamic rather than an abstract, independent roll for every single Lot in isolation. A patient bidder who lets others reveal their own hand on earlier Lots, saving their real intent for the one Lot they actually want, is playing a real, legitimate strategy this document explicitly supports.

---

## 6. Absentee Bidding — Attending Through an Agent

A household that can't or doesn't want to attend in person — managing a distant second settlement, or simply the paterfamilias occupied elsewhere — can bid through a trusted agent, most naturally a Steward or a dedicated Companions & Court Positions appointee, carrying explicit, pre-set bidding instructions (a maximum price, a priority ranking across several desired Lots). This is a real, honest tradeoff rather than a strictly worse option: an agent bidding on fixed instructions can't read the room the way a present principal can (missing the real momentum cues of §5), but frees the actual head of household from needing to physically attend every Auction Day a coveted item might turn up at.

---

## 7. Sub Hasta — War Spoils and Confiscated Property

A real, vivid, and genuinely attested ancient practice: property sold at a state-connected forced auction — war plunder, or goods confiscated from a convicted criminal or a proscribed enemy — was marked by literally planting a spear (hasta) at the sale site, giving the practice its own real Latin name, sub hasta ("under the spear"). This is the concrete mechanism behind Military & Combat's own War Spoils, when liquidated for the whole campaigning force rather than distributed directly, and Legal & Court's own confiscated property from a serious conviction.

---

## 8. Slaves at Auction — A Real, Distinct Acquisition Event

A real, higher-stakes alternative to Labor & Slavery's own calmer, ordinary market browsing, carrying real, felt drama precisely because a household competing for a specific person against a genuine rival bidder can be forced to pay well above what a quiet private purchase would have cost. Every one of Labor & Slavery's own Information & Deception mechanics and content-intensity provisions carries forward entirely unmodified.

---

## 9. Unique & Singular Lots

The real, dramatic mechanism the artifact trilogy (Books & Manuscripts, Art & Art Commissions, Masterworks & Unique Crafted Objects) and Hunts, Beast Taming & Menageries have all been missing since each first named "Purchase" as an acquisition path without specifying how one actually happens for something genuinely one-of-a-kind. A household can maintain a real, standing **Want List** for a specific kind of item, surfacing as a real Public Notice in the City Bulletin once a matching Lot is actually announced.

---

## 10. The Argentarius — Financing and Guaranteeing a Bid

Economy & Finance's own existing banker role gets a real, direct new use: an Argentarius can extend a short-term loan specifically to let a household bid beyond its own immediate liquid Treasury, or act as a guarantor vouching for a bidder's own creditworthiness to a wary seller.

---

## 11. The Ring — Buyer-Side Collusion

New this pass, and the honest, dishonest option every real auction house has always had to guard against: a group of bidders can secretly agree in advance **not** to bid against each other on a specific Lot, letting one of them win it artificially cheap, then privately split the savings or the item's own later resale value among themselves. Mechanically, this is a real, concrete Scheme (Characters §10) — genuine Progress, real Discovery risk scaling with how many co-conspirators are actually in on it, and real Counter-play if the seller or a shut-out rival bidder grows suspicious of an oddly quiet Lot. A discovered Ring is a real, serious Legal & Court matter and a genuine, shared Scandal for every household involved, not just whoever organized it — a direct, real echo of Business Competition's own Cartel mechanic (§4 of that document), now applied to a single Auction Lot rather than an ongoing market.

---

## 12. Puffing — Seller-Side Shill Bidding

The real, symmetrical mirror of §11: a seller secretly arranges for a confederate — a friend, a hired agent, or simply someone owed a favor — to bid on the seller's own Lot without any real intention of winning it, purely to drive up the final price a genuine buyer pays. This is a real, historically and legally recognized form of auction fraud (still illegal by that same name in real modern auction law), and this document treats it exactly that seriously: a discovered Puffing scheme voids the sale under the same real Legal & Court warranty logic §14 already establishes, and is a genuine, felt Scandal for the seller specifically, distinct from and often worse than an ordinary failed sale.

---

## 13. Post-Reserve Negotiation

A real, practical resolution for a Lot that narrowly fails to meet its Reserve Price: rather than automatically withdrawing it for a later Auction Day, the seller can choose to privately negotiate directly with whoever placed the top (short) bid — a real, plausible, lower-friction alternative to re-running the whole public event, available at the seller's own discretion rather than as a guaranteed fallback.

---

## 14. Buyer's Remorse and Fraud

- **The Winner's Curse.** A household that wins a hotly contested bid, particularly one pushed up by real Influence-spending or a Ring/Puffing scheme it doesn't even know is happening, can genuinely overpay.
- **Fraud, Discovered After the Sale.** A misrepresented slave, a forged ArtworkPiece or Masterwork, an undisclosed defect, a discovered Ring, or a discovered Puffing scheme all give the buyer real Legal & Court recourse, routed through the Auction's own seller or Praeco as the liable party.

---

## 15. The Praeco's Cut

A small, real, honest addition: the Praeco doesn't work for free — a standing commission, a real percentage of the final sale price, is this document's own concrete income source for that role, feeding directly into Notable Businesses or Companions & Court Positions depending on whether the household maintains its own retained Praeco or hires one ad hoc per Auction.

---

## 16. Cross-System Integration

- **Public Contracts & Competitive Bidding:** this document's own direct structural sibling, reusing that document's bid-evaluation formula wholesale.
- **Labor & Slavery:** the Slave Market Auction Day is a real, distinct competitive alternative to that document's own existing stock, with all Information & Deception and content-intensity provisions carried forward unmodified.
- **Military & Combat / Legal & Court:** sub hasta is the concrete liquidation mechanism behind War Spoils and confiscated property.
- **Economy & Finance:** Bonorum Venditio resolves Insolvency into a concrete event; the Argentarius gets a real financing use; the Praeco's Cut is a real, new income line.
- **Business Competition:** the Ring is a direct, real echo of that document's own Cartel mechanic, applied to a single Lot.
- **Characters:** the Ring and Puffing are both new, concrete Scheme types running on that document's existing engine.
- **Rival Houses / Succession & Dynasty:** an extinct House's own unclaimed holdings are a natural Estate Liquidation source.
- **Books & Manuscripts / Art & Art Commissions / Masterworks & Unique Crafted Objects / Hunts, Beast Taming & Menageries:** all four finally get a real, dramatic mechanism behind their own previously-vague "Purchase" acquisition path.
- **City Bulletin:** a Standing Want List surfaces as a real Public Notice.
- **Companions & Court Positions:** Absentee Bidding is a direct, real new use for a Steward or agent.
- **Notable Businesses:** the Praeco is a light, real, hireable role, now with a defined income source.

## 18. Open Questions

- **All numeric sizing**, per convention — Reserve Price-setting logic, sequential momentum's own magnitude, Ring/Puffing discovery-risk curves, and the Praeco's own commission rate are all unsized.
- **Auction frequency and advance notice**, still unresolved from the first pass.
- **Whether Absentee Bidding (§6) should carry any real, additional cost** for the convenience, beyond simply missing §5's own momentum cues.
- **Cross-reference with Discovery Races.** Whether a Unique Lot bidding war should share any mechanical DNA with Technology & Discoveries' own Discovery Race beyond the general competitive spirit isn't formally resolved.
