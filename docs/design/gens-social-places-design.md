# GENS — System Design: Social Places (§6.55, new)
*Polish and expansion pass. A real, missing middle layer between two things this project already built well: Villa's own private rooms (a controlled, invited guest list) and the Activity Engine's own deliberately convened gatherings (a real guest list, real Phases, real stakes). This pass adds three further real venues (the Thermopolium quick-service food counter, a real, famous Pompeii archaeological find; the elite strolling colonnade, or ambulatio; and the Harbor docks), a real Common/Fine venue-quality tier reusing Buildings' own existing pattern, a genuine Private Hire option bridging this tier directly into the Activity Engine, a concrete petty-crime risk at rougher venues, spontaneous Scenes tying Events' own content pool, and a light, direct connection to Organic & Autonomous Growth so a rival's own public appearances can actually surface as real gossip.*

## 1. Scope & Role — The World Outside the Villa and the Activity

Three real resolution shapes now exist for how Characters actually meet each other: a **Group Interaction** is quick, routine, and requires everyone already be gathered somehow; an **Activity** is deliberate, guest-listed, and carries real stakes precisely because it's been convened on purpose. Neither covers the single most common real form of ancient social life: simply going about a settlement's own public spaces on an ordinary day and running into people. This document is that third, lighter tier — visiting a real, named **Social Place**, with a real, semi-random chance of who else happens to be there, and a lighter, more casual band of the Interaction Catalog available once you are.

This document doesn't build new physical structures — every venue below is already a real Buildings §4.10 entry, or a light, natural addition to that same civic roster. It builds the social life that happens inside them.

## 2. The Social Place Registry

Eleven real, historically attested venue types:

| Social Place | Real Grounding | Distinct Social Function |
|---|---|---|
| **The Public Baths (Thermae/Balneum)** | Real Roman bathing culture, one of the very few settings where senator and common citizen used the same facility at the same time | The great cross-class equalizer, informal political networking, real Disease & Public Health hygiene benefit |
| **The Barbershop (Tonstrina)** | Real, proverbially talkative venue, genuinely a place to hear the latest news | The single best venue for Correspondence & Letters' own gossip channel and low-cost Espionage information-gathering |
| **The Tavern (Caupona/Popina)** | Real, ordinary lower-class social venue | Wandering Populations' own natural venue, informal wagering, real Crime & Punishment content |
| **The Thermopolium** *(new)* | A real, famous archaeological fixture — a counter-service hot-food-and-drink shop, distinct from a full sit-down Tavern, genuinely ubiquitous in Roman streets | A faster, more casual, more universally-affordable social stop than the Tavern — the natural venue for a brief, low-commitment encounter rather than a lingering one |
| **The Forum** | Real central civic and commercial heart of any Roman settlement | Informal political networking, the real physical posting site for the Acta Diurna |
| **The Macellum (Covered Market)** | Real daily food and goods market | A direct Familia tie for a household's own women having real, independent public social agency |
| **The Portico (Ambulatio)** *(new)* | A real, genuinely attested elite Roman leisure practice — a deliberate, unhurried stroll through a covered colonnade, valued for both exercise and conversation | A distinctly more elite, unhurried register than the Baths' own broader cross-class mixing — the natural venue for a real, deliberate, private-feeling conversation held in a technically public space |
| **The Gymnasium/Palaestra** | Real Hellenic institution, most concentrated in the Greek East | Education & Culture and light Military & Combat training flavor, real historically attested romantic and social context handled with the same indirect care as elsewhere |
| **The Circus/Amphitheater Concourse** | The real social space surrounding an actual Games & Spectacle event | Genuine cross-class crowd mingling, a real venue for public mood to pressure Politics & Patronage |
| **The Harbor Docks** *(new)* | A real, genuine maritime social hub — sailors, merchants, and travelers from across the known world passing through | A real, cosmopolitan register distinct from every other venue on this list — the natural place to meet a Wandering foreigner, hear news from distant regions, or encounter Language & Literacy's own Interpreter Problem directly |
| **Public Latrines (Foricae)** | Real, genuinely surprising ancient fact — real surviving graffiti attests to conversation there | A light, humanizing, low-stakes gossip and health-flavor venue |

## 3. Venue Quality — Common and Fine

New this pass, reusing Buildings' own existing tier pattern (the Bathhouse → Grand Baths chain) rather than inventing a parallel one: any Social Place can exist at a **Common** or **Fine** quality tier, tracking the underlying building's own tier directly. A Fine-tier venue — the Grand Baths rather than an ordinary Bathhouse, an elite Portico rather than a modest one — skews its own Social Encounter roll (§4.1) toward more prominent, higher-standing company, at the real cost of being a less plausible place to casually cross paths with a Wandering stranger or a household of modest means. Common and Fine aren't a strict hierarchy of "better" — they're simply different social pools, consistent with this project's own "no dominant setting" instinct.

## 4. The Social Encounter — A Third, Lighter Resolution Tier

Visiting a Social Place is a real, quick action, resolving in two steps:

### 4.1 Who's There

A small, semi-random roll (one to three Characters), weighted by the venue's own real social character and its Quality tier (§3). The roll can surface an existing family member or Companion, a Rival House member, a total stranger triggering lazy instantiation, or, honestly, nobody worth noting at all.

### 4.2 What You Can Do There

A genuinely lighter band of the Interaction Catalog than an Activity's own Phases permit: conversation, a compliment or light insult, an informational exchange, a Flirt, or a quiet first step in a Scheme's own information-gathering stage. The higher-stakes Interactions an Activity specifically unlocks remain that tier's own territory.

---

## 5. Cross-Class Mixing — Where Boundaries Genuinely Blur

The one real, distinct feature no other venue in this project offers: a Social Place is the one setting where an encounter between two Characters whose Legal Status or Dignitas gap would make a private meeting implausible can happen anyway — a citizen and a freedman sharing a bathhouse, an errand-running household slave and a Decurion's own wife crossing paths at the Macellum. Some of this project's own most interesting Character connections are far more plausible originating here than at a curated dinner.

---

## 6. Frequenting — A Household's Own Social Reputation

A Character who visits the same Social Place regularly can develop a real, light standing reputation there — a small, real bonus to that venue's own encounter quality, balanced against a small, real Dignitas or Scandal-adjacent read depending on which venue and how it's perceived, reusing existing Trait and Scandal machinery rather than inventing a parallel reputation track.

---

## 7. Private Hire — Bridging Into the Activity Engine

New this pass, and a genuine, practical historical option: a wealthy household could, and often did, arrange a private bath session, reserve a Tavern's own back room, or otherwise pay to exclude the ordinary public from a Social Place for a set occasion. Mechanically, this is the direct, real bridge between this document's own light tier and the Activity Engine (§6.47): a **Private Hire** converts an ordinary Social Place visit into a small, real Activity in its own right, with a genuine Host, a deliberate Guest List, and the higher-stakes Interactions an Activity's own Phases permit — the natural, lower-overhead alternative to a full Feast for a household that wants a controlled, private conversation without the expense and formality of hosting one at the Villa itself.

---

## 8. Petty Crime — A Real, Concrete Risk

New this pass, and a genuine Crime & Punishment tie rather than pure flavor: a Social Encounter at a Common-tier Tavern, Thermopolium, or the Harbor Docks specifically carries a real, small risk of petty theft — a purse cut, a pocket picked — resolved through Crime & Punishment's own existing machinery rather than a new one. This is deliberately rare and minor, never enough to make visiting a Social Place feel like a trap, but real enough to give a Fine-tier venue's own higher cost a genuine, felt safety tradeoff alongside its social-quality one.

---

## 9. Spontaneous Scenes

A Social Place visit can occasionally trigger a genuine, contextual Personal-scope Event (Events §2–3) — a public argument breaking out, an interesting stranger arriving with real news, a street performer drawing a crowd — the same real anchor-point logic the Activity Engine's own Phases already use for Events, applied here at a lighter, more spontaneous register befitting an unplanned visit rather than a hosted occasion.

---

## 10. Regional Variants

Light, flavor-only variation: the Gymnasium is the Greek East's own flagship Social Place; the Gallic Frontier's own public tavern-and-mead-hall equivalent is the natural public counterpart to Villa's own private Gallic Feasting Hall; Egypt's own real public bathing and market customs carry their own distinct regional color at the same venue types.

---

## 11. Cross-System Integration

- **Buildings:** every Social Place is an existing or lightly-extended §4.10 civic structure; Venue Quality (§3) reuses that document's own tier pattern directly.
- **Activity Engine:** Private Hire (§7) is a direct, real, deliberate bridge between this document's own light tier and that document's full Activity shape.
- **Characters:** the Interaction Catalog and Group Interaction's own aggregate shape are both reused for the Social Encounter (§4).
- **Correspondence & Letters / Espionage:** the Barbershop, Forum, and Harbor Docks are all concrete, physical venues for gossip and information-gathering.
- **Crime & Punishment:** Petty Crime (§8) is a real, new, concrete stakes source.
- **Events:** Spontaneous Scenes (§9) is a direct, new anchor point for that document's own Personal-scope content pool.
- **Politics & Patronage:** the Forum and the Circus/Amphitheater Concourse both provide informal networking and crowd-pressure venues.
- **Romance, Sexuality & Lineage:** the Baths, the Portico, and the Gymnasium all carry real historical romantic and social context, handled indirectly throughout.
- **Disease & Public Health:** the Public Baths carry a real, direct hygiene benefit.
- **Food Culture / Notable Businesses:** the Macellum and the Thermopolium are both natural, shared venues.
- **Familia:** the Macellum is a direct tie to a household's own women having real, independent public social agency.
- **Wandering Populations / Language & Literacy / Diplomacy with Non-Roman Peoples:** the Harbor Docks are this document's own most cosmopolitan venue, and its most natural home for the Interpreter Problem.
- **Games & Spectacle:** the Circus/Amphitheater Concourse is the real social space surrounding an actual event.
- **Technology & Discoveries:** the Forum is the Acta Diurna's own real, physical posting site.
- **Organic & Autonomous Growth:** a Rival House member's own visible presence at a specific Social Place is a natural, light, new gossip signal that document's own World Tick can surface without requiring full simulation depth.
- **Scandal:** Frequenting (§6) is a real, light, additional exposure and reputation surface.
- **Education & Culture:** the Gymnasium carries that document's own training and cultural-prestige flavor.

## 13. Open Questions

- **All numeric sizing**, per convention — encounter-roll weighting, Frequenting thresholds, Petty Crime odds, and Private Hire cost are all unsized.
- **Visit frequency and cost**, still unresolved from the first pass — whether visiting a Social Place carries any real time/opportunity cost distinct from an ordinary Group Interaction.
- **NPC-initiated Social Encounters at full simulation depth.** §11's Organic & Autonomous Growth tie establishes the concept, but the exact mechanism for how often and how visibly this surfaces isn't specified.
- **Whether a Spontaneous Scene (§9) can ever escalate into a full Activity on its own**, the mirror case to Private Hire's own deliberate escalation — left as a plausible future refinement.
