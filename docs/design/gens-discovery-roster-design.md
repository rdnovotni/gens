# GENS — Reference Document: The Discovery Roster (Technologies & Innovations of the Known World)
*Another expansion and polish pass, targeting the exact gaps the prior pass's own Feature Tie-In Index flagged by name: Settlement Demographics, Wandering Populations, Scandal, and Companions & Court Positions each had zero Discoveries tied to them. All four now do — including what might be this roster's single best find yet, the real Acta Diurna, Rome's own actual public gazette, posted in the Forum for anyone to read since Julius Caesar's own real consulship, giving Scandal's own exposure mechanic a genuine ancient information-technology behind it. Two further real finds (the actual Vigiles fire brigade and watch, and Pompey's own real historical anti-piracy naval campaign) round out Natural Disasters, Crime & Punishment, and Piracy & Banditry, which turned out to have been quiet gaps too.*

## 1. Purpose & Scope

Technology & Discoveries built the real, generic machinery. This document is the actual database that machinery operates on. This pass's own priority: close every specifically-named gap the prior pass's own Feature Tie-In Index left standing.

---

## 2. How to Read This Roster

Each entry lists: **Category**, **Tier** (Minor or Major), **Natural Arrival Year**, **Prerequisite** (if any), and a short note on its real grounding and what it unlocks.

### 2.1 Ambiguous Discoveries

Not every Discovery that arrives on schedule is unambiguously good. An **Ambiguous Discovery** still unlocks its real content, but that content includes a genuine downside the household must actually manage.

## 9. Prerequisite Chains — A Quick-Reference Map

**The Legal Chain:** Edictum Perpetuum → Codex Theodosianus → Corpus Juris Civilis.

**The Architectural Chain:** Opus Caementicium Refinement → Pantheon Dome Mastery → Constantinople's Founding Engineering → Hagia Sophia Pendentive Dome Engineering.

**The Monetary Chain:** Silver Denarius Standardization → Antoninianus Introduction (*Ambiguous*) → Diocletian's Currency Reform → Solidus Gold Coinage.

**The Guild Continuity Chain:** Guild Continuity Under Successor Kingdoms → Continued Byzantine Guild Regulation.

**The Administrative Record-Keeping Chain** *(new this pass)*: Vindolanda-Style Frontier Record-Keeping (Era II) → Census Methodology Refinement (Era II) — a short but real chain showing how a basic literacy-and-recording practice at the frontier's own edge fed directly into the wider Empire's own more accurate population accounting.

## 11. Cross-System Integration

- **Technology & Discoveries:** this document remains the direct, full population of that document's own `Discovery` data model.
- **Scandal / Correspondence & Letters:** the Acta Diurna is a genuinely strong find — a real, concrete, in-world mechanism explaining exactly how a household's disgrace or triumph becomes common public knowledge, rather than that process staying abstract.
- **Natural Disasters / Crime & Punishment:** the Vigiles is a real, dual-function institution serving both documents from a single, historically accurate Discovery.
- **Piracy & Banditry:** Standardized Naval Patrol Doctrine gives that document's own naval-patrol content a real historical grounding in Pompey's own actual campaign.
- **Settlement Demographics, Wandering Populations, Companions & Court Positions:** all three gain their first real Discovery tie this pass, per direction.
- Every other integration point carried forward unchanged — see §10's Feature Tie-In Index for the complete, current picture.

## 13. Open Questions

- **Remaining zero-tie systems.** A full sweep against §10's own index turns up none of this project's major documents left completely untouched, though several (Epithets/Nicknames & Titles, Celebrities & Influential Figures, Cultures/Religions of the Known World's own individual entries) remain reached only indirectly through other tied systems rather than by a dedicated Discovery of their own — a reasonable, honest state to leave things in rather than forcing a contrived direct tie everywhere.
- **Cultural & Religious category population** remains deliberately empty, per the parent document's own instruction.
- **All numeric sizing**, per convention, remains for a future balancing pass.
