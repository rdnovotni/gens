# GENS — System Design: Coming of Age & Childhood Education (§6.59, finalized)
*Expansion, polish, and finalization pass. Education & Culture already built the real investment mechanics of a Roman childhood — the Pedagogy pipeline, the Rhetoric/Philosophy/Gymnasium tracks, Institutions of Renown, literacy. Familia and Traits already own Formative Trait acquisition during Childhood and Adolescence. What none of them ever built is the real, dateable ritual scaffolding an actual Roman childhood passed through: a real naming-day ceremony, a real protective amulet, the real day formal schooling starts, and the toga virilis itself — this project's fourth full Activity Type. This closing pass adds the toga praetexta's own real protective social status, the real Liberalia festival date the ceremony was often (though not exclusively) tied to, and — the single most useful addition this pass makes — the real tirocinium apprenticeship a young Roman man actually entered immediately after the ceremony, shadowing a senior mentor in either public life or the army before his own career properly began.*

## 1. Scope & Role — What Already Exists, and What's Missing

Education & Culture's own Pedagogy pipeline (§3 of that document) and Educational Tracks (§4) remain exactly as designed — this document touches neither. What it adds is the real ritual and curricular scaffolding that always surrounded that investment without ever being built: a real naming ceremony at birth, a real childhood amulet, the real day formal schooling actually starts, the real three-stage curriculum sequence preceding Adolescence's own Track choice, the toga virilis itself, and — new this pass — the real apprenticeship immediately following it.

---

## 2. The Dies Lustricus — A Real Naming-Day Ceremony

A real, genuinely attested Roman purification and naming ceremony, held on a fixed, real, gendered schedule: the eighth day after birth for a girl, the ninth for a boy. At this ceremony, the infant formally receives its praenomen (Familia's own naming convention, §2.8 of that document), a household Lares-adjacent religious rite (Religion §2-3) is performed on the child's behalf, and the child receives its first protective amulet (§3). This is a light, intimate, Quick Activity (Activity Engine §3) — family-only, low Scale, no real Quality-tier drama, but a genuine first milestone worth marking.

---

## 3. The Bulla and the Lunula — A Child's First Heirloom

Two real, distinct, gendered protective amulets: a boy received a bulla — a real, round or heart-shaped locket, worn from the dies lustricus until his own toga virilis (§6) — and a girl a lunula, a real, crescent-shaped amulet, worn until marriage. A wealthy household's own Bulla or Lunula can be a genuine, named Heirloom Jewelry Masterwork (Masterworks & Unique Crafted Objects §3) — dedicating this specific, storied item at the child's own coming-of-age moment (§6, §8) is a real, emotionally concrete use of that category: a family retiring a piece it may have watched several of its own children wear in turn.

---

## 4. Starting School — A Real, Concrete Milestone

Real, attested Roman custom placed the start of formal schooling around age seven. This document treats that real transition as the actual, concrete trigger point for Education & Culture's own Pedagogy pipeline (§3 of that document) to formally begin, rather than that pipeline starting at some undefined moment during undifferentiated "Childhood." A modest, real Household Affairs-tier moment for the City Bulletin, not a full Activity, but a genuine, dateable event.

---

## 5. The Real Curriculum Stages — Litterator, Grammaticus, Rhetor

A real, three-stage ancient curriculum sequence, giving the years before Education & Culture's own Track choice real historical texture without altering what happens after it:

- **The Litterator** (or Ludi Magister) — basic reading, writing, and arithmetic, roughly ages seven to eleven.
- **The Grammaticus** — deeper grammar and genuine literary study, roughly ages eleven to fourteen, including real, attested close study of major poets — a direct tie to Graffiti's own Literary Quotation category (§2 of that document): the same lines an educated adult later scrawled on a wall were, plausibly, the ones drilled into them at exactly this stage.
- **The Rhetor** — formal oratory training beginning around fourteen, feeding directly into Education & Culture's own Rhetoric Track (§4 of that document) once Adolescence's Track choice activates.

## 6. The Toga Virilis — A New Activity Type

The real Roman ceremony marking a young man's formal transition to legal adulthood and citizenship, built as this project's fourth fully-specified Activity Type:

1. **Host** — the father or head of household.
2. **Type** — "comingOfAge".
3. **Venue** — the household's own Atrium for the ceremony's first Phase, then a real procession to the settlement's own Forum.
4. **Guest List** — family, patrons, and clients, carrying the same guest-list politics Weddings already established.
5. **Duration** — Quick, typically a single day, extended slightly by a culminating Feast.
6. **Phases** — below.

### 6.1 Dedication of the Bulla

The young man formally removes the bulla worn since infancy and dedicates it to the household Lares — the exact moment a storied Bulla (§3) either becomes a retired family Masterwork treasure or, for a modest household, simply a small, sincere observance.

### 6.2 The Robing

Dressing for the first time in the plain, unbordered adult toga virilis, replacing the toga praetexta of childhood. Worth naming directly: the toga praetexta itself carried a real, attested protective social status — a child wearing it was understood to merit a real degree of social protection, including a real, documented informal custom of avoiding crude language in its wearer's presence. The Robing is therefore a real two-sided moment: gaining adult standing, and quietly losing a real, specific social protection that came with visible childhood.

### 6.3 The Forum Procession

A real, public procession to the Forum, where the young man is formally presented — a genuine, visible Dignitas-scaling event reading Activity Engine's own Witness Pool (§7 of that document) directly, the same real logic already governing a Wedding's own deductio.

### 6.4 The Feast

A nested Feast Activity (§6.48), Purpose extended to include a coming-of-age celebration, following the exact nesting pattern Weddings §3.7 already established.

### 6.5 Liberalia — A Real, Common Calendar Date

A real, specific historical detail worth including directly: the ceremony was often, though never exclusively, held on March 17th, the real festival of Liberalia, honoring Liber Pater — a real Roman god associated with freedom and wine, and a thematically fitting real date for a ceremony about a young man's own new freedom and standing. A household can choose to hold the ceremony on this real date specifically, tying it directly into Religion's own Sacred Calendar (§5 of that document) for a modest, real Favor and Dignitas bonus for the deliberate alignment — or hold it at another time entirely, since real Roman practice was genuinely variable rather than fixed.

Real historical timing for the ceremony itself varied — commonly mid-teens, inconsistent across families and eras — and this document deliberately doesn't fix a single number, per convention.

## 7. The Tirocinium — A Real Apprenticeship After the Ceremony

New this pass, and the single most useful structural addition here: the *toga virilis* was never simply an ending. Real Roman practice immediately followed it with a genuine apprenticeship — the **tirocinium fori** for a young man entering public life, or the **tirocinium militiae** for one heading toward a military career — shadowing a senior, established figure to learn the actual, practical business of politics or war firsthand before his own career properly began.

### 7.1 The Mentor

This document ties the Tirocinium directly to Characters' own existing relationship web: the mentor is a specific, named Character — most naturally a father's own close friend, an established Patron (Politics & Patronage), or a senior commander (Military & Combat) — rather than an abstract institution. A real, earned Mentor/Student bond (Familia §2.7) forms directly from this period, one of the most durable and consequential relationships a young Character can carry into adulthood.

### 7.2 Real, Concrete Payoff

A completed Tirocinium Fori grants a real, direct head start into Politics & Patronage's own cursus honorum — a young man who spent real time actually watching the Forum's own business conducted up close enters his first magistracy bid with a genuine, earned advantage over one who didn't. A completed Tirocinium Militiae grants the equivalent real head start into Military & Combat's own officer track. Either path is optional, and a Character who skips it entirely isn't blocked from either career — they simply start from the ordinary baseline rather than a real, earned advantage.

## 8. Coming of Age for Girls — An Honest Asymmetry

Worth stating plainly rather than inventing a false parallel: Roman culture did not provide girls with a distinct, standalone coming-of-age ceremony comparable to the toga virilis, and it correspondingly offered no real equivalent to the Tirocinium either — a young woman's own path to adult social standing ran through marriage (Weddings, §6.49) rather than a public apprenticeship, a real, honest reflection of the period's own gender roles rather than an oversight this document should paper over.

What is real and worth adding: a real, attested, genuinely poignant practice of a bride dedicating her own childhood toys and her Lunula (§3) to a goddess — most commonly Venus, or the household Lares — shortly before her wedding. This document adds that as a real, small addition to Weddings' own Sponsalia Phase (§3.1 of that document) rather than building a separate Activity Type around it.

## 9. Childhood Play — Toys and Light Flavor

No new mechanic, purely real, humanizing texture: real, attested Roman children's toys and games — dolls (tied directly to §8's own dedication practice), hoops (trochus), and knucklebones (astragali, a real ancient dice-and-chance game played by children and adults alike, a light precursor to Games & Spectacle's own adult wagering culture).

## 10. Formative Traits — Real Anchor Points

Familia and Traits' own Formative Trait rolls (§2.6 of the former, §5 of the latter) now explicitly fire at this document's own real milestones rather than floating free — the dies lustricus and early Childhood for an innate-leaning early read, Starting School (§4) for a Character's own first real response to formal structure, and the Toga Virilis, its own Tirocinium (§7), or a girl's own Betrothal (§8) for how the young adult actually meets their new formal status — the same "give an abstract process a felt anchor point" logic the Activity Engine's own Phases already gave to Events.

## 11. Cross-System Integration

- **Education & Culture:** the Pedagogy pipeline and Educational Tracks remain entirely unmodified; §5 gives the years preceding Adolescence's own Track choice real, dated texture.
- **Activity Engine / Feasts / Weddings:** the Toga Virilis is this project's fourth fully-specified Activity Type; §7's Tirocinium is a real, new post-ceremony structure feeding directly into two other documents.
- **Politics & Patronage / Military & Combat:** the Tirocinium Fori and Tirocinium Militiae both grant a real, concrete head start into the cursus honorum and the officer track respectively.
- **Characters / Familia:** the Tirocinium's own Mentor bond is a real, durable Mentor/Student relationship-web addition; Formative Trait rolls (§10) now fire at real, named milestones.
- **Masterworks & Unique Crafted Objects:** the Bulla and Lunula are real, concrete Heirloom Jewelry use cases.
- **Religion:** the dies lustricus, the Dedication of the Bulla, and Liberalia (§6.5) are all real, direct Sacred Calendar and Lares-tied ties.
- **Graffiti, Dynamic Walls & Rumors:** the Grammaticus stage's literary study directly precedes that document's Literary Quotation category.
- **City Bulletin:** Starting School and a Toga Virilis are natural Household Affairs entries; a prominent family's own ceremony is plausible Headline material.
- **Games & Spectacle:** astragali is a light, real precursor to adult wagering culture.
- **Weddings:** §8's toy-and-Lunula dedication is a direct addition to that document's own Sponsalia Phase.

## 13. Open Questions

- **All numeric sizing**, per convention — exact age ranges, Tirocinium duration, and the real magnitude of its own career head start are deliberately left unfixed.
- **Whether a delayed or unusually early Toga Virilis should carry its own real social reading.**
- **Cross-cultural variance.** Non-Roman cultures within Cultures of the Known World plausibly had their own distinct coming-of-age practices; this document builds only the Roman version.
- **Whether Starting School (§4) should ever fail to happen at all** for a household too poor to afford even a Litterator, and what real consequence that gap should carry.
- **Whether a Tirocinium's own Mentor bond should carry any real risk** if the mentor later falls from grace, dies, or becomes a Rival — a plausible, dramatic future refinement not resolved here.
