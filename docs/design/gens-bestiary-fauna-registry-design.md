# GENS — Reference Document: The Bestiary (Fauna of the Known World)
*Final polish pass. A standalone, authoritative registry, built in the same spirit as Resources & Goods (the authoritative registry for trade goods) and Cultures/Religions of the Known World (the authoritative registry for peoples and faiths) — but for animals and creatures. Livestock, wild game, exotic imports, marine life, birds, venomous pests, and mythological creatures are all consolidated here into one central source. This closing pass adds a third, deliberately lighter tier sitting between "confirmed ordinary animal" and "confirmed Legendary creature" — real ancient travelers' rumors of strange beasts that this document keeps honestly unresolved, led by the genuinely real, documented account of Hanno the Navigator's own "Gorillai" — and a short, honest note on what this registry deliberately leaves out and why.*

## 1. Purpose & Scope

Every animal-adjacent mechanic in this project so far has invented its own small, local list rather than drawing on a shared one. This document is where all of that consolidates into one real, comprehensive, cross-referenced registry — the single place any future document should check before inventing a new creature, and the single place that already-scattered content now formally lives.

This document does not change any existing mechanic. Resources & Goods' own livestock yield math, Hunts' own Game Tier difficulty and Combat Resolution Engine treatment, and the Menagerie's own Notable Specimen system all remain exactly as designed — this is the shared reference layer underneath all of them.

## 11. Legendary & Mythological Creatures

Per Hunts, Beast Taming & Menageries §6's own explicit, named departure from this project's usual historical-grounding default, this document is the canonical descriptive source for every Legendary entry — Hunts itself owns the combat mechanics, capture rules, and Notable Specimen treatment; this document owns what each one *is*.

### 11.1 Unique Individual vs. Legendary Species

Some myths describe one single, permanently unique creature (there is only ever one Nemean Lion, one Hydra, one Minotaur), while others describe what's really closer to a rare species, with the real, live possibility of more than one individual existing. This document marks each entry accordingly, and Hunts' own Repeat Encounters mechanic (§11 of that document) reads this flag directly: a Unique Individual that's genuinely slain or permanently captured is gone from the world for good; a Legendary Species entry can still plausibly turn up again as a different individual even after one has already been dealt with.

### 11.2 Encounter Type

Not every Legendary creature is a straightforward fight. Each entry carries its own natural **Encounter Type**:

- **Combat** — a direct, dangerous physical confrontation.
- **Riddle/Wit** — resolved through Learning, Rhetoric, or cleverness rather than force.
- **Lure/Hazard** — a passive or semi-passive danger, most naturally threatening a Ship or Travel Party.
- **Capture-Only/Benevolent** — a creature that isn't hostile by nature, offering no meaningful "kill" reward.
- **Nature-Spirit Encounter** — a lighter, stranger meeting, resolved more like a Diplomacy negotiation or an Omen than a fight.

### 11.4 Rumored & Unconfirmed — A Deliberately Lighter Third Tier

New this pass, and distinct from both the ordinary fauna above and the confirmed Legendary roster: a real, genuinely fun category for further real ancient travelers' claims this document keeps honestly ambiguous rather than either dismissing or confirming. The single best real example, and this tier's own namesake case: the real historical Carthaginian explorer Hanno's own surviving account of a voyage down the West African coast describes his party encountering hairy, aggressive figures his interpreters called "Gorillai" — a real, genuine ancient text, and, remarkably, the actual real etymological origin of the modern word "gorilla." This document doesn't resolve what Hanno's party actually saw (a real, unfamiliar primate species, a garbled account, deliberate embellishment) any more than the real historical record does. Any future rare, remote-frontier rumor in this same honest, unresolved spirit — a further strange sighting reported by a distant trader or a Travel encounter, never confirmed and never mechanically real the way a §11.3 entry is — belongs here rather than being promoted into the confirmed roster.

## 12. Notable Absences — What's Deliberately Not Here

A short, honest note on real gaps, so a future reader doesn't mistake an absence for an oversight:

- **The silkworm.** Resources & Goods already tracks Silk as an imported good, but the actual silkworm and the closely guarded Chinese sericulture process that produced it remained genuinely, almost entirely unknown to the Roman world for this entire game's own historical range — a real, honest "known unknown" this registry preserves by simply not inventing a creature entry for it, rather than an oversight.
- **A single, all-purpose generic "monster."** Every entry in §11 is a specific, named, real mythic figure rather than an interchangeable fantasy stock creature, deliberately, to keep this document's own Legendary tier feeling like real mythology rather than an ordinary fantasy-game bestiary wearing a Roman coat of paint.
- **Invented species with no real ancient basis.** Everything in this document — ordinary, exotic, and Legendary alike — traces to a real animal, a real ancient account, or a real, attested myth. Nothing here was made up from nothing.

## 14. Cross-System Integration

- **Resources & Goods:** every Livestock and Exotic Beasts entry is this document's own direct expansion of that document's existing, more compressed roster.
- **Hunts, Beast Taming & Menageries:** every Game Tier example and all seventeen confirmed Legendary creatures are drawn directly from this registry; §11.1's Unique/Species distinction and §11.2's Encounter Type both directly inform that document's own mechanics; §11.4's Rumored tier is this document's own supply of future rare Travel-encounter content that never needs to resolve into a confirmed Hunt target.
- **Games & Spectacle:** the Venatio's own beast roster draws directly from §6's Exotic & Imported Beasts.
- **Religion:** the sacred chicken and raven/crow's own Auspices roles, the cat's Egyptian Bastet association, the Egyptian crocodile-Sobek tie, the owl's Minerva association, and the dolphin's own real Omen association are all direct, concrete links to that document's own machinery.
- **Villa:** the Columbarium, Menagerie/Aviary, Ostium (Guard Dogs), and Private Dock/Boathouse (Moray Eels) all draw their real population directly from this registry.
- **Food Culture:** the edible dormouse's own real *glirarium* practice and the nightingale's own curiosity-food status are both direct, concrete additions to that document's own real-historical-excess content.
- **Masterworks & Unique Crafted Objects:** the eagle's own direct tie to the Military Standard category, and scorpions/asps' tie to the Poison Ring category, both draw on this registry.
- **Disease & Public Health:** venomous creatures, fleas/lice, and vermin are all real, direct hazard sources.
- **Natural Disasters:** locusts and weevils are both natural, direct additions to that document's own existing crop-failure and stored-goods-loss causes.
- **Private Ships & Shipping Ventures:** the dolphin's voyage-luck association, the Sea Serpent, Sirens, and Scylla and Charybdis are all direct, concrete maritime ties.
- **Military & Combat:** the war elephant is this registry's own note on a real historical military use distinct from the Exotic Beast's more common civilian/spectacle role.
- **Diplomacy with Non-Roman Peoples:** Satyrs and Fauns' own Nature-Spirit Encounter type is a natural, light extension of that document's own negotiation-flavored machinery.
- **Travel:** §11.4's Rumored tier is this document's own direct, ready-made source of rare, atmospheric encounter content.

## 16. Open Questions

- **Whether this registry should eventually assign a numeric Rarity or base trade value to every entry**, or leave that entirely to Resources & Goods and Hunts per their own existing sizing conventions.
- **Full regional coverage.** §13's map covers every region with a genuinely distinctive fauna entry; a region not listed simply uses the generic Common/Dangerous Game and ordinary Livestock rosters.
- **Whether Falconry deserves fuller treatment** as its own light Hunt sub-type in a future revision.
- **How a Riddle/Wit or Nature-Spirit Encounter Type should actually resolve mechanically** — §11.2 names the category; the specific resolution is left for Hunts' own future revision.
- **How large the Rumored & Unconfirmed tier (§11.4) should eventually grow**, and whether any given entry in it should ever be allowed to "graduate" into a confirmed Legendary entry through sustained play — left open rather than decided either way.
- **New-creature intake process.** As future documents are written, this registry should be the first place checked and the place any new animal gets added — worth stating as a standing house convention rather than leaving implicit.
